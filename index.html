LAB COAT

A lab coat is a special clothing worn in laboratories by scientists, students, and technicians. It is designed to protect the wearer from chemicals, spills, and dirt.

The lab coat is usually white. It is made of light and strong fabric, such as cotton or polyester. It has long sleeves that cover the arms. It has a collar and buttons in the front. It usually has three pockets: two on the lower part and one on the chest.

The lab coat is loose so it is comfortable to wear. It helps keep our clothes clean and protects our skin from dangerous substances.

In conclusion, a lab coat is very important in the laboratory. It is not only a part of safety, but also a symbol of professionalism and responsibility.
